01. Dual-License Framework

LDOC Studio is governed by a transparent dual-license model designed to protect open scientific interoperability while providing industrial guarantees for enterprises:

  • The Open Living Document Standard (Apache 2.0): The file format container (PKWare ZIP), manifest.json schema, and pure arithmetic text measurement architecture are open and unrestricted for independent implementation.
  • LDOC Studio Workstation & Cloud (Proprietary Commercial): The GPU rendering pipeline, visual presentation studio, AI copilot, and enterprise fleet installers are licensed under commercial terms.

02. Enterprise Fleet SLA Commitments
